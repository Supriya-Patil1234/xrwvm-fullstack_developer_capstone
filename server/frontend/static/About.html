About Us

Welcome to Best Cars dealership, home to the best cars in North America.

Founded in 2005, Best Cars has grown from a single showroom in Chicago into a network of more than 40 dealerships across the United States and Canada. We sell new, certified pre-owned and imported vehicles at honest, reasonable prices, and every customer review helps us improve.

Our promise is simple: transparent pricing, no hidden fees, and a team that treats you like family. Meet the people behind the dealership below.
